## Framing

**Eloquii — From first interest to completed order**

The existing case study explains the modular platform and internal marketing operations. This companion explains the customer journey: maintain interest, support the product decision, preserve browsing context, and carry information into checkout. In-house development ties those decisions to cost management and control over promotions and iteration.

The copy uses the owner's account and supplied screens. It does not claim measured conversion lift, revenue attribution, research sample sizes, or specific test results. Mobile and iOS continuity comes from the owner's account; the supplied assets show desktop only. “Outcome” describes the delivered experience, not a quantified business result.
